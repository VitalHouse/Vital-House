VITAL HOUSE — WEBSITE UPLOAD PACKAGE
====================================
This package is a complete, stand-alone static website for GitHub Pages.

IMPORTANT: Upload ALL files INSIDE this ZIP to the ROOT of the GitHub repository.
Do NOT upload the ZIP itself. Do NOT upload an enclosing folder.

The original logo artwork was not redrawn. The "original-logo-exact.jpeg" is a
byte-for-byte copy of the supplied logo. The transparent variant is derived
from those same original letterforms to blend into the cream page background.
Photos of vials are carefully cropped directly from the supplied real-product
photograph (not AI-generated replacements).

HOW TO UPLOAD
1. Make a local backup of your current repository if desired.
2. Unzip this package on your computer.
3. Go to https://github.com/VitalHouse/Vital-House
4. Click Add file > Upload files.
5. Drag all 14 files listed above from the extracted folder onto the upload area.
6. GitHub will replace files with the SAME NAMES. Commit the changes.
7. Open https://www.vitalhouse.org/ and refresh the page. If needed, use a hard
   refresh to clear your browser cache.
8. You may remove older, unused images or asset folders later. They won't
   interfere with this version. Do NOT delete the CNAME file.

KEY FEATURES
- Homepage closely follows the supplied Vital House three-panel mockup.
- Research catalog: all 15 products with general research descriptions.
- Shop: 15 product cards, original price, 25% discounted price, quantity
  selection and ADD TO CART.
- Cart: line-item editing and removal, original totals, discount amount,
  discounted subtotal, estimated Nevada tax by selected county, and total.
- Contact inquiry: required name, email, phone; optional organization and
  additional questions. Opens the visitor's own email app (mailto:) addressed
  to vitalhouseresearch@gmail.com. Visitor must SEND it; it is NOT automatic.
- Alternative "Copy inquiry details" action for visitors with no email app.
- No card details or payment collection. Inquiries are nonbinding.
- Cart persists in visitor's browser via localStorage.
- Responsive layout: desktop, tablet, and mobile.

IMPORTANT LIMITATIONS
- This static site CANNOT automatically email the business without an external
  form/email service. A mailto link only opens an email draft for the visitor
  to review and send. The copy button is the fallback.
- Sales-tax rates are estimates based on a user-selected Nevada county and
  assume a taxable transaction. The final taxable status and jurisdiction must
  be determined before a legally permitted transaction.
- Listing a research compound does NOT imply authorization to sell or ship it.
  Have a qualified legal professional review all product listings, buyer
  eligibility, labels, claims, and policies before facilitating transactions.
- Avoid presenting lifestyle photography as evidence of product health effects.
- No payment gateway or server database is included.

If images or styling do not show, verify ALL files are at the same repository
root level: https://github.com/VitalHouse/Vital-House
